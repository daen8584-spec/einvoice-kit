1. What einvoice-kit is

An app for Shopify stores that issues invoices and credit notes from orders and refunds, numbers them, prepares the Verifactu records and lets the store download everything. It's built by an independent developer (contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]).

By installing the app and accepting these terms in it, the store accepts these terms and the data processing agreement in section 8.

2. What the app does, and what it doesn't

  • It checks every invoice before issuing it. If something doesn't add up (a mistyped tax ID, for example), it holds the invoice without numbering it and says why.
  • It doesn't send records to the Spanish tax office yet. Until it does, the store must meet that obligation by other means when it applies. That's why invoices don't carry the Verifactu QR code yet.
  • It isn't tax advice. The store is responsible for its data (VAT rates, tax details, exemptions) and for checking its invoices are correct.

3. Availability

The app is provided as is. It may have interruptions and may stop working at any time; where possible, stores will be told in advance. No issued invoice is lost because of an interruption, but no new invoices are issued while the app isn't running.

4. Your copies

The law requires the store to keep its invoices. The app doesn't guarantee to keep them. To help, it copies each invoice into its Shopify order (a private field, "Facturas (einvoice-kit)"), and that copy belongs to the store and stays even if it uninstalls the app. Even so, the store must keep its own copies: the "Download everything" button provides them as PDFs, a spreadsheet and the Verifactu records. When the app is uninstalled, everything the app stores is deleted after 48 hours; the copies in the orders stay in the store.

5. Price

The app is currently free. If it ever has a price, that will be announced beforehand and charged only with the store's agreement, through Shopify.

6. Liability

As far as the law allows, the developer isn't liable for indirect damages, for data losses the store could have avoided by keeping its copies, or for penalties arising from incorrect data provided by the store or from obligations the app states it doesn't cover. None of this limits liability that the law doesn't allow to be limited.

7. Governing law and changes

These terms are governed by Spanish law. If they change, a new version will be published with its date and the app will ask for it to be accepted again.

8. Data processing agreement

This agreement meets Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Parties and subject

The store is the controller of its customers' data. The developer of einvoice-kit is the processor, and processes it only to issue the store's invoices and credit notes, prepare its Verifactu records and let it download them, for as long as the app is installed.

Data and people involved

The store's customers who place orders: tax ID (if given), billing name or company, billing address, and order details (products, amounts, taxes, dates). No email, phone or special categories of data are processed.

The processor's obligations

  • Process the data only on the store's instructions: those in this agreement and those it gives by using the app.
  • Keep it confidential, with access only for whoever needs it to provide the service.
  • Apply appropriate security: encrypted connections, signature checks on every Shopify notification, encryption of stored data and minimal retention (described in the privacy policy).
  • Use sub-processors only where needed for the service. Currently the server provider ([HOSTING PROVIDER]) and Cloudflare. The store will be told before any is added or changed, and can object by uninstalling the app.

The store's obligations
