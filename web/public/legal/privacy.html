Who runs the app

einvoice-kit is an app that issues invoices for Shopify stores. It's built and maintained by an independent developer. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]. Country: [COUNTRY].

Who decides about the data

  • The store's customers' data (as it appears in orders): the store is the controller. einvoice-kit processes it only on the store's behalf, to issue its invoices, under the data processing agreement.
  • The store's own data (its domain and the invoicing details it enters): einvoice-kit is the controller and uses it only to provide the service.

What data the app uses

FromWhat
Shopify orders and refundsOrder number and date, products, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes and shipping. About the customer: their tax ID if they give one at checkout, the billing name or company, and the billing address (street, city, postal code and country).
The storeThe store's domain, the invoicing details it enters in the app (company name, tax ID, address, time zone, exemption text) and an access permission from Shopify.

The app does not use customers' email addresses or phone numbers, uses no tracking or analytics cookies, and builds no profiles. In the browser it only remembers the chosen language.

What for

Only to issue the store's invoices and credit notes, prepare the Verifactu records, save a copy in each of the store's orders, and let the store download them. Nothing else: no advertising, no selling data, no sharing it for other purposes. The only thing the app writes to Shopify is that copy.

How it's protected

  • Connections with Shopify and with the store's admin are encrypted (HTTPS), and every Shopify notification is checked against its signature before it's used.
  • Stored invoices, drafts and notifications are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Shopify's access permission is stored encrypted too.
  • Shopify's notifications are emptied as soon as they're processed and deleted after 30 days.

How long it's kept

For as long as the store has the app installed. When it uninstalls, Shopify asks for deletion 48 hours later, and the app then deletes everything about that store: invoices, records, invoicing details and permissions.

The app doesn't keep a store's invoices long term. The law requires the store to keep its invoices for years. So the app copies each invoice into its own Shopify order (a private field called "Facturas (einvoice-kit)" that only the store can see), and that copy belongs to the store and stays even if it uninstalls the app. The store can also download everything at any time.

Providers involved

Your rights

If you're a store's customer, exercise your rights (access, correction, deletion and so on) with the store: it decides about your data, and einvoice-kit will help it. Note that the store may have to keep its invoices by law. When Shopify passes on one of these requests, the app logs it with the order numbers involved only.

If you're a store, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your country's authority.
